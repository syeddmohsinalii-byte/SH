Our Story

How We Became "The Paras of Gulshan"

For decades, art students and painters living in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Johar, and Malir had to travel miles across town to Saddar (Paras Art) or Urdu Bazaar just to buy heavy watercolor paper, oil tubes, and drafting tools.

SH. SAEED changed that forever. By stocking authentic imported brands like Cadence, Pebeo, Winsor & Newton, Faber-Castell, and German UHU — right next to NIPA Chowrangi on Rashid Minhas Road — we brought the entire Urdu Bazaar & Saddar experience closer to home, at fair, competitive rates.
