## 🚀 Website Pages & Features

### 1. `index.html` (Master Portfolio & High-Converting Retail Funnel)
* **Hero Banner:** "Karachi's Biggest Retail Stationery & Fine Art Hub", trust badges (40+ years in business, 4.6★ rating, 15,000+ items).
* **Live Store Status Pill:** Real-time calculation based on Karachi timezone (GMT+5) showing whether the shop is Open, Paused for Juma prayer, or Closed.
* **Store Atmosphere Carousel:** Real photos of the storefront, aisles, paint racks, and easel displays.
* **Why Karachi Chooses Us:** Explains the "Paras of Gulshan" positioning — Urdu Bazaar and Saddar pricing without cross-city traffic.
* **Quick WhatsApp Order Widget:** Instant quote and product inquiry.
* **Verified Customer Review Snippets:** Featuring actual quotes from local guides (Faiz Bhai and Mehtab Bhai mentions).

### 2. `products.html` (Complete Searchable Catalog & Filterable Shop)
* 40+ popular items across 6 core categories:
  1. **Fine Arts & Paints:** Cadence Acrylics, Marie's Oil Paints, Winsor & Newton, Pebeo, Mod Podge.
  2. **Canvases & Surfaces:** Stretched cotton canvases (12x16 to 24x36), canvas boards, round MDF panels.
  3. **Brushes & Studio Tools:** Filbert taklon brushes, 000 micro liners, palette knives, beechwood easels.
  4. **Papers & Sketchbooks:** 300gsm cold-pressed watercolor pads, 150gsm cartridge sheets, toned tan pads.
  5. **Craft & Architecture:** Balsa wood, foam boards, cutting mats, German UHU adhesives, craft beads.
  6. **School & Office Stationery:** Sakura Pigma Micron pens, Faber-Castell pencils, Casio ClassWiz calculators.
* Live search and sort by price / rating.
* "Add to Cart" and direct "Quick Buy on WhatsApp" for every product.

### 3. `sketchbook-builder.html` (Interactive Custom Sketchbook Atelier)
* Highlighted directly from customer reviews (*"They make custom sketchbooks for me with my own choice of paper..."*).
* Step-by-step interactive configurator:
  - Step 1: Dimensions (A5 Pocket, A4 Portfolio, A3 Studio, Square 8x8, Square 12x12).
  - Step 2: Paper Grade (300gsm Cotton Watercolor, 150gsm Cartridge, 250gsm Mixed Media, Toned Tan, Black Velvet).
  - Step 3: Page Count (40, 60, 100, 160 pages).
  - Step 4: Binding (Hardcover Wire-O, 180° Lay-Flat Stitched, Linen Cloth Hardbound).
  - Step 5: Custom Cover Inscription / Name Stamping.
* Live interactive visual preview card with dynamic price calculation in PKR.
* 1-Click export to WhatsApp message formatted directly for Faiz Bhai.

### 4. `reviews.html` (Wall of Love & Verified Google Reviews)
* 4.6★ rating overview with 5-star distribution breakdown bar charts.
* All 18 verified customer reviews extracted directly from Google Maps with reviewer names, Local Guide level, star rating, dates, and full comments.
* Interactive search and category filtering ("Fine Arts", "Custom Sketchbooks", "5-Star Only").
* Direct link to leave or view Google Maps reviews.

### 5. `about.html` (40-Year Heritage & Karachi Art Story)
* The history of SH. SAEED serving Karachi's art landscape since the 1980s.
* Meet Faiz Bhai & Mehtab Bhai (praised for patience and demonstrations).
* Institutions served: IVS (Indus Valley), KU Visual Studies, NED Architecture, Habib University, Beaconhouse, DUET.

### 6. `contact.html` (Store Directions, Karachi Map & Delivery Calculator)
* Exact directions from NIPA Chowrangi, Rashid Minhas Road, and Gulshan Block 6.
* Embedded responsive Google Maps frame.
* Operating hours timetable and Namaz prayer etiquette notice.
* Karachi Rider delivery guide (Bykea, InDrive, Yango) and nationwide courier.

### 7. `cart.html` (Order Checkout & WhatsApp Message Generator)
* Full persistent cart table synced via `localStorage`.
* Delivery area dropdown in Karachi (Gulshan, Johar, PECHS, Clifton, DHA, Nazimabad, etc.).
* Automated structured WhatsApp message generator formatted with customer name, phone, items, delivery choice, and total in PKR.
